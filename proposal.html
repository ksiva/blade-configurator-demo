Proposal: the full Resonant configurator

01. What the demo shows

Geometry from your Flared.step at true size: blade 151 × 258 × 6 mm and both half handles. Wood on the blade and Handle 1 is your own photography, aligned to the CAD outline. Handle 2 covers only the same area as Handle 1, and its wood and end cap are built from your photograph: the oak face is taken directly from it, and the padauk grain from the part of the handle that is in focus. Free 360 degree rotation and zoom, including the handle end. The options, prices and weights are working samples of the final system. Only PALIS with the Flared handle is real 3D today. Tested in desktop and phone-sized browser windows, not yet on a physical phone.

02. Technology

three.js (WebGL) with physically based materials, in any modern browser, nothing to install. No subscription, no outside service. In the final version: a WordPress plugin with a shortcode, its own admin pages, and WooCommerce cart integration.

03. Adding new handle designs later (your PDF, section 3)

  • Do it yourself in WordPress, no coding: a design is a base wood, up to 6 straight veneer stripes (position, width, wood), an end cap wood and a rim. You pick from your wood library and see the result live. Limit: straight stripes and caps only. Curved inlays, chevrons and burl need the photo route.
  • Photo route, we prepare it: you send straight-on photos of a finished handle (both sides and the end) and the Fusion 360 shape. We align them to the shape and add the design to your library. About two hours of work per design. The price per design is agreed once the workflow is set, before the extra package of 10 designs is quoted.
  • New wood species: two photos, front and back, as you planned. Each becomes a reusable material for all models.

04. Image export

PNG in the browser, up to 4096 px, transparent or white background, any configuration and view. No separate render step needed. A separate high-quality render mode can be added later without slowing the normal page.
